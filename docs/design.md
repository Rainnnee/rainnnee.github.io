# Design notes

## Purpose and content

The page introduces Rain Piao to recruiting, academic, and general visitors through a single-page portfolio. It opens with an animated name over the supplied skyline, then settles into a compact banner and a portfolio with education, combined research and project experience, skills, personal interests, music, and profile links. Project cards open focused detail pages. The academic and project details are based on information Rain provided; the site leaves out private contact details and GPA.

The About heading displays Rain’s supplied Chinese signature below the small “A personal portfolio” label. A native SVG viewport crops only the surrounding whitespace of the supplied JPEG, and an SVG luminance filter makes the white paper transparent while preserving the handwriting. The handwriting is not generated or redrawn. Reading mode displays the plain name instead. The document title and accessible heading remain the plain name. The visual wordmark uses **ℝain ℙiao**. Education lists the two B.S. programs Rain specified. Pennington School is omitted until there is a specific achievement or detail that merits the extra entry. The Music section includes two user-supplied tracks with custom waveform scrubbers and browser audio playback. The Immersive music section presents those tracks directly without a separate “selected work” panel. Reading mode hides Music and the personal-interest section to keep the page CV-like.

Research and projects share a single horizontally scrollable Experience gallery in Immersive mode. On wide screens, the taller cards show two projects at once with a small peek at the next; medium screens show one full card and a peek, while narrow screens use a swipeable single-card view. Each card has a clear kind label (research, student project, team competition, trading competition, or independent project). Visitors can swipe, scroll, use the previous/next buttons, or use the keyboard to browse; the gallery does not auto-advance. Reading mode turns the same content into a compact vertical list. A separate Skills section uses native expandable groups for math and statistics, computing and data, machine learning research, and algorithms. This keeps academic subjects visible without mixing them into the personal-interest section.

## Two presentation modes

The default Immersive mode uses the supplied skyline at its original colors as a vivid masthead and as a soft background behind translucent reading panels. Navigation overlaps the masthead. A live CSS `backdrop-filter` layer uses a broad gradient mask that follows the top edge of the page content below the navigation. The navigation keeps its own glass treatment; the background blur and a light wash move with the content edge, gradually filling the screen as the page scrolls without switching suddenly to a fully blurred state.

On each page load in Immersive mode, one fixed sky image stays in place. The name begins appearing immediately at screen center by resolving from blur to clear and sliding upward, while its underline grows outward from the center at the same time. The reveal takes about two seconds; as soon as it finishes, the masthead contracts, carrying the name into the small top banner as the page content rises into view from below. The title begins moving into the banner at about two seconds; the full sequence takes about 3.8 seconds and never zooms the name. Immersive mode disables browser scroll restoration for that load and starts at the top, so refresh replays the opening consistently; section links return to their target when the sequence finishes. The navigation and blur boundary move together, gradually placing the blurred layer beneath the navigation. The intro runs in Immersive mode on each page load; Reading mode is the user-selected still presentation. The page is not locked during the sequence: scrolling, clicking, tapping, Tab, or Escape dismisses it. A small oval down-arrow appears when a mouse pointer reaches the bottom edge; touch screens get the cue after the intro.

One two-position switch changes between Immersive and Reading modes. At intermediate screen widths, the navigation links move to their own row while the mode switch stays beside the name. Reading is a deliberately calmer, denser layout: white background, familiar serif type, compact spacing, plain panels, and no opening or scroll animation. It shows education, experience, and expandable skills as a compact CV-like page; its navigation omits the hidden Music and personal-interest sections. The selected mode is saved locally and shared with project detail pages; Immersive is the default. Keyboard focus, skip navigation, clear headings, and readable contrast are available in both modes.

## Name and palette

**ℝ** nods to the real numbers; **ℙ** nods to probability. The blackboard-bold initials give the name a mathematical accent while the other letters remain familiar. A thin underline spans the wordmark. Its navy center covers about 62% of the name width, with longer transparent fades at both ends so the line emerges softly from the sky.

| Token | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#173F70` | Wordmark, headings, and primary text |
| Warm white | `#FFFAF7` | Reading surfaces and glass panels |
| Pale sky | `#D8EDFA` | Sky-toned fallback |
| Pink | `#EFACD0` | Underline, focus, and small accents |
| Cyan | `#59DEFF` | Ambient light in the immersive header |
| Lilac | `#DFD3FF` | Project-card accent |

The image provides most of the page’s brighter blue and pink in Immersive mode, supported by soft cyan and lilac color fields. Education and project cards use individual pastel tints so the information remains easy to scan. The About heading uses a self-hosted Latin subset of Caveat 500 for a consistent handwritten look without implying an actual signature. Its source is the [Google Fonts Caveat family](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/caveat), and the font’s OFL license is included at `assets/fonts/OFL-Caveat.txt`. Reading mode uses a familiar local serif throughout.

## Artwork and inspiration

`assets/images/sky-full.png` is the 3344 × 1880 PNG supplied by Rain on 2026-10-13. It is stored byte-for-byte, without lossy compression or resizing. `assets/images/sky-preview.jpg` is a 1600 × 899 preview displayed immediately while the full PNG loads; the crisp image fades in once the browser has decoded it. The preview uses the same crop as the full image. No Xeuphoria album artwork is distributed with this site.

The About section uses two supplied portraits: `assets/images/rain-piao-pier-selfie.jpg` (1200 × 900) in Immersive mode and `assets/images/rain-piao-sunset-portrait.jpg` (900 × 1200) in Reading mode. Both keep the photographs’ natural color. Immersive uses the pier selfie in a landscape frame, preserving Rain’s left-of-center position and room around the outstretched hand; Reading uses the golden-hour portrait in a tighter vertical crop. The original uploads are not included in the repository. The Immersive running card includes the 1080 × 2341 Apple Watch workout screenshot supplied by Rain. CSS crops it within a restrained portrait preview while adjacent text repeats the distance, workout time, and pace so the key facts do not depend on reading the screenshot.

`assets/audio/breath-away.m4a` and `assets/audio/camelia-overdose.m4a` are AAC listening copies made from the WAV tracks Rain supplied. The masters remain outside the repository. The players use `preload="none"`, so audio is downloaded only when a visitor starts playback. Precomputed SVG waveforms are seekable; native audio controls remain available when JavaScript is disabled. Durations are 4:40 and 2:39.

The project cards link to static detail pages in `projects/`. Each page summarizes only the role, methods, and outcome currently supplied for that project and provides a path back to Experience. The ACM AI Poker Decision Model page is marked in progress and describes the project question—estimating fold, call, and raise probabilities from the current game state and observed opponent behavior—without implying completed results. The Immersive interest board has separate cards for music, running, chess, VALORANT, CFan Theatre, and SouLA A Cappella. It shows Rain’s approximate Lichess blitz rating, VALORANT current and peak ranks, CFan acting credits and sound/music contributions, and SouLA vocal and production roles. The CFan and SouLA cards link to their public Instagram accounts. The VALORANT rank badges are prominent above their written rank names; CFan’s winter skit is listed as 《安可》 and 《生死客》 is identified as a fall play.

Interest-card icon provenance: `assets/images/lichess-favicon-128.png` comes from the [Lichess logo assets](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/public/logo/lichess-favicon-128.png); `assets/images/valorant-logomark-red.png` comes from Riot’s [VALORANT press assets](https://www.riotgames.com/en/press); the Platinum 3 and Diamond 1 badges are Riot game assets served through the [community-maintained VALORANT API](https://valorant-api.com/v1/competitivetiers); `assets/images/cfan-theatre-logo.jpg` and `assets/images/soula-logo.jpg` are the public profile icons from [CFan Theatre](https://www.instagram.com/cfanucla/) and [SouLA A Cappella](https://www.instagram.com/soulaacappella/), respectively. ## Updating the site

Keep content in semantic HTML and styles in `assets/styles/`. `assets/scripts/main.js` handles the main page’s two presentation modes, opening sequence, scroll cue, current section indication, and content-following blur ramp. `assets/scripts/project-detail.js` keeps the mode preference available on project pages. Keep asset URLs relative so they work beneath `/rainpiao/` on GitHub Pages. Record any replacement artwork’s source and provenance here, and do not add album artwork used as visual reference.
