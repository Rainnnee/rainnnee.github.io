Finding small unfamiliar regions in images

Paper submitted to ALT · awaiting decision

I work with a five-person team on training-free methods for detecting small out-of-distribution regions using frozen CLIP patch features. I extended a Python Monte Carlo study across 225 experimental settings and evaluated a spatial-scan method that improved simulated detection power under Gaussian-mixture noise.
