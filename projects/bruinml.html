Finding unfamiliar regions in images.CLIP-Based Out-of-Distribution Detection

I worked with a five-person BruinML team on finding small out-of-distribution regions in images using frozen CLIP patch features.

The research question

A small unfamiliar region can disappear when an image is summarized as a whole. We study how to detect these local regions without retraining CLIP, using its patch-level features.

My contribution

I extended a Python Monte Carlo study across 225 settings: five noise distributions, five object geometries, and nine signal strengths. I compared global, maximum, top-k, and spatial-scan detection methods.

What the simulation showed

In one Gaussian-mixture setting, the spatial-scan approach increased simulated detection power from 19.9% to 97.7% at a 5% false-positive target. Those numbers describe that setting, rather than a result across every experiment.

Manuscript

I coauthored the manuscript. The research work is complete. We submitted the manuscript to ALT 2027 and are awaiting a decision.
