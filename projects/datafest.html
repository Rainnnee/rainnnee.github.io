Making sense of healthcare data

A 48-hour team analysis of de-identified patient data, ending with recommendations for cancer care.

Project snapshot

Role
Team coordinator
Team
Five people
Tools

Approach

Our team explored relationships among stress, age, encounter volume, and social needs in a de-identified patient dataset. I coordinated the five-person team as we worked through the analysis and prepared a final presentation.

We used R, visualizations, and rank correlation to examine the data, then presented recommendations for cancer care.
