ASA DataFest at UCLA · May 2026

A dataset, five people, 48 hours.Healthcare Data Analysis

I coordinated a five-person team working with de-identified patient data at ASA DataFest.

Role
Team coordinator
Team
Five people
Tools
R, ggplot2, Spearman correlation

Working with the data

We cleaned and filtered the data, then looked at relationships among stress, age, encounter volume, and social needs. We used ggplot2 to visualize the patterns and Spearman correlation to examine the relationships.

What we found

In the dataset, younger patients and people reporting food, financial, or transportation needs tended to report higher stress. We used these associations to inform recommendations for cancer care.

Making it understandable

We brought four analyses together in a five-minute, two-slide presentation. I coordinated the team through the analysis and the final presentation.
