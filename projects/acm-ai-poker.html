The decision problem

Poker decisions depend on both what is happening now and what has happened before. The project explores how to represent the current game state alongside behavior observed across earlier hands, then estimate the probability of actions such as folding, calling, or raising.

This is a model of decision-making under uncertainty: the context informs a distribution over possible actions rather than assuming every situation has one obvious answer.
