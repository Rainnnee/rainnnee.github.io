Turning sheet music into MIDI.PDF-to-MIDI Conversion — Pusheng

I’m building a tool to turn printed sheet music into playable MIDI and editable MusicXML, starting with piano and standard single-instrument scores.

Status
In development
Current work
Recognition & evaluation
Formats
PDF → MusicXML → MIDI

Where I am now

An offline pipeline runs optical music recognition, compares recognized notes with reference scores, and exports MIDI. I’m comparing Audiveris and HOMR and checking pitch, note placement, duration, and score structure separately. Clean examples work; real scores still expose substantial recognition errors.

The musician’s side

The goal is to put the original score beside the recognized notation, let musicians review and correct mistakes, then listen and export their work. That upload-and-review interface is still ahead. I also want the eventual playback to account for dynamics, articulation, and tempo markings, with uncertainty made visible.
