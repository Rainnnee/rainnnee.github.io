Independent project · The Pennington School · 2025

From point clouds to passing cars.LiDAR Traffic-Monitoring System

I built a C++ pipeline that groups LiDAR points into vehicles and tracks them over time to estimate traffic speed.

Tools
C++ & Linux
Method
3D breadth-first search
Setting
Eight-lane roadway

From points to vehicles

I used a sensitivity-tuned, three-dimensional breadth-first search to group nearby LiDAR points and build bounding boxes around the resulting vehicle clusters.

Tracking motion

The pipeline tracks the boxes across frames. I estimated vehicle speed from three-dimensional displacement over elapsed time to support traffic-flow analysis.

Guidance

I worked on the project while at The Pennington School, with guidance from Gang Zhou, a Principal Software Engineer at Seyond.
